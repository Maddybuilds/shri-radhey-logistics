About Shri Radhey Logistics

Building on a legacy of excellence in logistics and transportation.

Over 15 years of experience in logistics and transportation, built on a strong foundation through three companies — Shri Logistic and Courier Services, Lakshmi Express, and Transworld Cargo Movers. Having successfully catered to diverse client needs with reliability, efficiency, and commitment, we have now established a new private limited firm, Shri Radhey Logistics Pvt Ltd, with an initial capital of ₹20 lakhs — driven by a vision to expand across India and beyond.
